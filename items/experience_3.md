# Competitive Swimming

## What was it that you did?
I was a competitive swimmer throughout high school.

## When did you do it?
Through high school, until 2022

## What was good about it?
It gave me an outlet while I was living at boarding school.

## What was your favourite memory of it?
Winning my first medal. I had been discouraged from competing in primary school, so earning it meant a great deal.

## What didn't you enjoy about it?
The 3am training sessions.

## Grade
**C**: I'm proud of what I achieved, but the early mornings kept it from being more enjoyable.
