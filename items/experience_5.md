# Volunteering at a Children's Home
## What was it that you did?
I volunteered at a children's home for babies and toddlers under 5, playing with the children and helping take care of them.

## When did you do it?
2019

## What was good about it?
The children were adorable, and playing with them and caring for them was fun.

## What was your favourite memory of it?
Holding the newborns for the first time. They were usually quarantined for their health, so it felt special.

## What didn't you enjoy about it?
Toddlers are messy, and cleaning up after several of them at feeding time was exhausting.
